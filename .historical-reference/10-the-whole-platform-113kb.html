  6. Working context · Doma — One context can be assembled within a size budget; combining several contexts is incomplete.

  7. Yours to keep — Seity archive export exists. Full human binding and export of vault keys remain unfinished.
    STACK I §§2,5; II §§2–3; X §1 · SHIPPED / PARTIAL / GAP
  8. One conversation — A global chat panel and Telegram components exist; the continuous desktop experience is incomplete.
    STACK V §4; XII §§1,4.14,5 · PARTIAL
  9. Answer panel — No answer panel, artifact record or locked-down panel frame is built.
    STACK XII §4.14; XI §I · GAP

    Chapters, images, diagrams, decks, short video and interactive pages are views onto the Writing Desk store. Edits reach the Assistant before its next reply. Publishing is your explicit choice. Generated media is saved immediately to your storage with its provenance. Generated code is isolated without network access by default, cookies or credentials.

  10. Voice — No customer dictation, spoken conversation or readout is built.
    STACK XII §4.13; XI §I · GAP

    On-device speech is preferred wherever it meets the quality bar. Provider speech goes through the platform under zero-retention terms. Transcripts carry the source “the person’s own words”. Narration of finished work is a separate creative use.

  11. Its own browser — No estate browser, login broker, per-grant compartments or browser receipts are built.
    STACK XII §4.12; XI §I · GAP

    Signing in there is consent. Hosted sessions stay in the vault; local sessions stay on your machine. Receipts keep the domain and action, never the page. Unknown outcomes are not automatically retried. A repeated task pauses if its outcome check fails.

  12. Freedom dial — Permission profiles exist; the three positions are not mapped or connected to the Assistant.
    STACK XII §§4.1,5–6 · SHIPPED — staging only / GAP
  13. Approval cards — Cards exist for the Director. Per-person delivery, expiry and resuming approved work have gaps.
    STACK XII §§4.1,5–6 · SHIPPED — Director only / GAP
  14. Reviewer in shadow — A laboratory reviewer exists; its shadow seat in the Assistant is not built.
    STACK XII §§4.2,6 · GAP

    Shadow use comes first. It can only approve what the dial permits. Turning on automatic approval requires a later decision after real-use evidence; completion of this map does not grant that authority.

  15. Your readings — The sketch object is built; conversation wiring is held and is not authorized to proceed.
    STACK XII §§4.6,5–6 · PARTIAL / GAP

    A plain execution task can skip the sketch. Rejected readings stay in the Assistant’s room and do not become memory without affirmation. The person closes by being ready, not by selecting a winning reading.

  16. Habits & watches — Personal routines, event triggers, read logs and unattended memory access remain unbuilt.
    STACK XII §§4.5,4.12,6 · GAP
  17. Your connected sources — Gmail and Drive reads exist; Calendar, write-back and Assistant-specific grant controls are incomplete.
    STACK XII §§4.3,5–6 · SHIPPED / PARTIAL / GAP
  18. Writers Factory — Writing methods and a making screen exist; the named room entrance is absent from Sakme.
    STACK I §3; V §1; XI §V · GAP — Sakme entrance

    The practices index records the making screen and version preservation as shipped. The absence in Part V concerns the Sakme entrance, not all writing capability in the estate.

  19. Evidence Foundry — A separate research product is documented; its Sakme entrance and making-method integration are gaps.
    STACK I §3; V §1; XI §V · GAP — Sakme entrance

    The making-rooms index names research-facility as the product and marks the creative method within Evidence Foundry as an open gap. Research claims remain subject to warrant; they cannot be made true by a personal ruling.

  20. Workshop — Video and making-screen components exist; the complete room is not established by STACK.
    STACK I §3; XII §4.6 · PARTIAL

    The practices index names the video lab and a keep/reopen method. It records wildcard generation, preserved versions and the human-selection screen as shipped. This is not evidence that the whole room is complete.

  21. Developer Room — A governed-code frontend exists; its backend connection is not confirmed in Sakme.
    STACK V §3; VI §7; XI §V · PARTIAL
  22. One-person company — A paused sketch with no product repository; no completed company room is documented.
    STACK I §3; Appendices E · DOCTRINE

    The making-rooms index explicitly describes this as a paused operations-room sketch, separate from the discontinued Solopreneur vertical.

  23. Your own labs — Existing labs are named, but a customer room builder is absent from Sakme.
    STACK I §§3,7; V §7; XI §V · GAP — customer builder
  24. The estate room — Operator grants exist; tappable waits, a waiting-on-me view and Assistant closure are missing.
    STACK XII §4.9 · SHIPPED / GAP
  25. Two moments are yours — Human selection and preserved variants are recorded built; the complete Assistant-run method is direction.
    STACK I §§1,3; V §4 · DOCTRINE

    The making-rooms index records the shipped selection screen. A decision records whose hand made it. Developer tasks after dispatch and company operations use execution rules, not an indefinitely open making process.

  26. Writing Desk — Contracts and a surface exist; the complete Desk and its storage foundation are not documented as delivered.
    STACK V §§4,9; Appendices E · PARTIAL

    The supplemental Writing Desk specification (§§0–1,4) identifies contracts, projection tools and a surface, with a build-status correction. The store remains on its own delivery plan; this diagram does not infer completion from a frontend.

  27. Your work store — The CTO’s storage foundation is recorded in progress; STACK did not verify its implementation.
    STACK V §§4,6,9; Appendices E · PARTIAL — in progress

    The dated appendix records the Desk store work as open. Its current delivery is not independently audited here. The Writing Desk specification §§4.2–4.5 describes assets, ordered projects, named versions and project history.

  28. Binder — The navigation tree and file browser are built. They are not the Writing Desk editor.
    STACK V §5 · SHIPPED
  29. Identity — Engine login and commercial-layer verification exist; tenant identity is separate from office grants.
    STACK III §1; I §7; X §2 · SHIPPED
  30. Memory — Memory and exemplar services exist; the full Assistant memory path remains incomplete.
    STACK II §2; XII §5 · SHIPPED
  31. Secrets · the vault — Credential custody exists; the full per-grant browser broker is not built.
    STACK II §2; XII §4.12; XI §I · SHIPPED / GAP
  32. Records · Notary — Signing and verification primitives exist; full production receipt coverage is missing.
    STACK II §2; IV §§1,5; XII §§5–6 · SHIPPED / PARTIAL / GAP

    The documents distinguish signed creative-decision streams from ordinary audit tables with unused hash fields. They do not establish estate-wide, signed production receipts. A source-specific primitive is not complete runtime coverage.

  33. Approvals — Separate approval mechanisms exist, but some permission routes fail open and approve-to-resume is missing.
    STACK II §2; XII §§4.1,6; XI §II · DEFECT / GAP
  34. Sharing · bonds — Bond records and signing identities exist; human counter-signing and assistant collaboration are incomplete.
    STACK I §1; II §§2–3; XII §4 · PARTIAL / GAP
  35. Messaging — Telegram components and a notification centre exist; unified channels and product-event wiring are incomplete.
    STACK III §7; XII §§1–3,5 · PARTIAL / GAP
  36. Model access — Funding and pin components are built; the bot uses platform models and ignores your settings.
    STACK XII §§4.8,5; XI §II · SHIPPED / DEFECT

    Funding order, difficulty routing and per-task pins exist as components. They are not a complete personal-Assistant path. Bringing the Telegram route onto that path—including masking—is engineering work.

  37. Privacy before models — Pattern masking exists; Assistant route wiring and provider-policy enforcement remain unfinished.
    STACK X §9; XII §§4.8,5; XI §II · PARTIAL / GAP

    Pattern masking does not anonymize prose: names and places are not covered. On a person’s own keys the provider terms are shown and the choice remains theirs. Managed use requires verified zero-retention terms.

  38. Standards & safe tools — Detection and prompt-only certification work; correction, tool certification and runtime isolation have gaps.
    STACK II §2; IV §§3–4; XI §§II,IV · SHIPPED / GAP
  39. Sign-in — Login handoff, identity verification and entry routing are implemented.
    STACK III §1; V §2 · SHIPPED
  40. Billing · SEIT — Checkout, subscription handling and the append-only credit ledger exist per person and product.
    STACK III §§3–4; VII §2 · SHIPPED

    Built refers to payment and ledger mechanisms, not complete usage settlement. Some inference paths still lack true-up to measured cost, and some engine usage rows do not carry actual cost.

  41. Limits & warnings — Spend-cap components exist; customer controls and the 80% warning are missing in the documented snapshot.
    STACK XII §§4.8,5–6; VII §2 · PARTIAL / GAP
  42. On your own machine — The same-build sovereign experience is direction. Centre memory location and sync are not yet designed.
    STACK I §§3,5; IX §1; XI §II · DOCTRINE / GAP
  43. Hosted for you — Managed services exist; equivalent complete rooms across both tiers are not established.
    STACK I §5; IX §§1,4 · SHIPPED / DOCTRINE
  44. Messaging side doors — Telegram is the first channel. Other side doors, account links and desktop return paths remain future work.
    STACK XII §§1–3,4.14,5 · PARTIAL / GAP
  45. Machine noise — Operational channels exist; moving the right decision cards into the Assistant remains unfinished.
    STACK XII §4.9; VI §§2,5; VIII §1 · SHIPPED / GAP
