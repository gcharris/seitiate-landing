APPROACH & ACCESS

The Porta & The Gate

At the top of the ridge where the orchards thin against ancient stone walls stands the heavy timber gate. Ivo holds his lantern. Above the arch, pressed into soft amber wax, is the Hallmark. No pedigree is checked; a first mark weighs the same as a master's, but the stone remembers who struck it.

I · THE HARBOR & THE GATE

Nobody checks your pedigree at this door

The stranger comes up the salt-white road from the Portus before dawn. The town thins into orchard, and the orchard thins into stone wall, and at the top of the road stands the gate.

"Nobody does, the first time. That's not a mark against you. Costs nothing to walk in unmarked. Nobody checks your pedigree at this door—a first mark weighs exactly the same as a master's here, which is the whole point of having a door at all instead of a wall with no opening in it. What you can't do is fake having earned one." — Ivo, Keeper of the Gate (A Dream of the Estate)

Above the arch is the Hallmark, struck in soft wax. In the old days, visitors struggled with names. Under the ratified two-body architecture, it is simple: The estate you enter is Sakme. Seitiate is the unseen engine under the floorboards—the cold pipes and background machinery that keep the rooms warm.

II · SCRIPTORIUM & THE DESK

The singular writing surface of the estate

Past the Barbican where the Notary sits, the Scriptorium smells of tallow and vellum. At the center of the hall stands the Writing Desk.

Before today's remediation, the estate suffered from duplicate furniture: the scholar sat with an unlinked manuscript, while over in the laboratory, the scientists had erected an awkward wooden <textarea> directly onto their test benches, trying to write sentences where they were supposed to be titrating chemistry.

That clumsy textarea has been dragged out and dismantled. The Writing Desk is now the singular writing surface across all wings. As the scholar drafts long-form prose, they make an inline claim mark: [warrant:ef_claim_4810a]. They do not re-litigate the science; they bind their sentence to a living claim.

III · THE RESOLVER SEAM

The courier in the cloister

Running between the Scriptorium and the science wings is a vaulted cloister: the Evidence Resolver Seam (GET /api/v1/evidence/resolve/:ref).

When the scholar's quill attaches an evidence mark, an errand-runner walks across the flagstones to the Evidence Foundry. The courier returns to the desk carrying a verified slip:

• Claim Status: grounded (attested by data, uncontested under ADR-001).
• Support Issuer: A Zenodo DOI, a Zotero research URI, or an Empirical Own-Measurement Warrant.
• Cryptographic Seal: SHA-256 fingerprint + root digest.

A reader touching the margin of the manuscript touches the full empirical weight of the laboratory.

IV · COLLEGIUM & THE HONESTY FLOOR

Calibrating your scale is not a scientific pass

Across the courtyard in the Collegium (Evidence Foundry) and the Sensorium (Sensarra), researchers run calibration curves, microfluidic experiments, and biosensing benchmarks.

Here, PR #227 buried the estate's most dangerous flaw: the verdict floor inversion.

Previously, running a method-validation benchmark against known synthetic test data would, upon clean execution, slap a green PASS on the research run. A software check was masquerading as empirical proof. The Master of the Bench has restored the honesty floor: running a test to check that the machine executes merely proves the software works—it remains strictly inconclusive. Only true failures ground an immediate fail; a scientific pass requires a sealed canonical evidence envelope signed by the Notary.

V · THE SILENTIUM & THE KEEP

The unrecorded sanctuary and the four floors of Seitas

In the shaded corner of the cloister lies the Silentium—the Vipassana-born room where nothing is recorded, no camera watches, and no receipts are minted. The estate's honesty is proven as much by what it refuses to surveil as by what it hallmarks.

Rising above the whole palace is the Keep (Seitas), four floors from stone to sky:

1. The Arca (The Vault): Deep in the bedrock. The strongbox of sovereign user keys. The user holds their own keys; if the estate falls, the user walks away intact.
2. The Horreum (The Granary): The ground floor. Everything a life gathers—notes, papers, readings—kept unsorted and unjudged.
3. The Proprium: The second floor. What is properly yours. Of everything gathered, what you affirm and stand behind.
4. The Summarium: The sunlit summit. The crystallization of years into essence.
