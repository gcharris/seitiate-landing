What you can build

Your work.
Your offering.
Your business.

The same foundation supports a person working on an idea, a group making something together, and a business serving its own customers. Your seity and Personal Assistant keep the person at the centre.

01 · YOUR OWN WORK

Think, make, and follow through.

Research a question, develop a manuscript, plan a project, or organise the moving parts of your day. Your Personal Assistant works from your seity—your context, taste, style, and standards—and helps keep tools and specialist agents focused on your intent.

02 · WORK WITH OTHERS

Bring people into the room.

Shared spaces bring colleagues, clients, and collaborators together. Each person brings their own perspective and assistant; the group can develop ideas, coordinate work, and create something together.

03 · CREATE & SELL

Give your expertise a market.

Offer a video channel, a course, a research method, a creative service, or an AI workflow. Set a price in SEIT, reach members who value your work, and earn from one-time purchases or ongoing subscriptions.

04 · BUILD ON THE FOUNDATION

Make an application of your own.

Build a specialist workbench, a creative studio, or a service for your community. Shared AI capabilities, assistants, workspaces, accounts, and commerce let you concentrate on the experience and expertise you bring.

05 · GROW A BUSINESS

Your identity. Your customers.

Operate an independent business on the same infrastructure. Bring your own brand and customer relationships, offer your own products and subscriptions, and participate in the wider marketplace.

Connected through SEIT

What you earn can keep working.

Spend earnings on another member’s tools or services, pay a collaborator, or redeem them for crypto. SEIT connects the work people do with the value they exchange.
