Five Steps · One Platform

From your first conversation to your own business.

Seitiate is designed to grow with you. Everyone starts with a Personal Assistant. Where you go from there—sharing with a team, publishing your expertise, building a product, launching a company—is up to you. The same platform and the same SEIT credit at every step.

01
For everyone

Your Personal Assistant

An assistant that genuinely gets to know you—your work, your preferences, your priorities. Not a blank slate every time you open it. A persistent partner that remembers what matters to you and picks up where you left off, every session, on the web, on your phone, or through smart glasses.

02
For teams & peers

Shared Spaces

Bring colleagues, friends, or clients into shared spaces where your assistants help you think, build, and decide together. Everyone keeps their own assistant, their own memory, their own perspective. You collaborate without merging.

03
For creators & specialists

Turn Your Expertise Into an Offering

Turn what you know into something the world can use. Whether it’s a video channel, a specialist course, a research methodology, or an AI workflow—publish it, price it in SEIT, and earn directly from members who value what you’ve built.

04
For product partners

Co-Build Your Application

Partner with Seitiate to bring your software idea or studio to life. Your application gets built-in AI assistants, shared rooms, and the entire SEIT economy—without building any of that infrastructure yourself.

05
For founders & businesses

Launch Your Own Business

Run a complete, independent digital business powered by Seitiate’s accounts, subscriptions, and credit economy. Own your brand. Own your customer relationships. We provide the infrastructure; you provide the vision.
