01 / Understanding

Less explaining again.
More moving forward.

Your seity is a person-owned intelligence that develops through your work together—shaped by your experience, judgment, taste, and corrections. Your Seitiate Assistant draws on it to carry your intent through the work.

You can inspect, challenge, and change what carries forward. What suited one project should not become a rule you have to fight on another.

01 / THE IDEA

Keep the possibility.

She mentions the idea on a walk. She is thinking aloud; nothing needs to start yet. When she returns to it, the questions are still there.

02 / THE FIRST CUT

Make intent visible.

She chooses a pilot, a scope, and a budget. A first cut meets the technical requirements, but its interpretation misses something important.

HER CORRECTION
“The images are right, but you have made the people sound nostalgic. I want them to sound alive.”

Her correction changes the work—and informs what comes next.

03 / THE NEXT CUT

Let experience matter.

In the next cut, the people’s present-day ambitions take centre stage. She can see how her correction changed the film and refine what carries forward.

03 / Participation

The foundation is shared.
The direction is yours.

She might share the pilot privately. She might collaborate on another film, offer a workshop, or build an independent business. Each is a worthwhile direction.
