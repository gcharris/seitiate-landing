Phases and the first test

Policy precedes dependent gates; supply and evidence closures precede activation. Cost and follower work may proceed independently.

  1. Policy. Charter/tier rules first, then gate code; the gate packet records the charter tier-map prerequisite.
  2. Supply primitives. Confirm published main claim commits bound to run, base and expiry; build scope refusal and harness binding; include shared coordination writes. Role Domas keep knowledge; sessions claim per run.
  3. Evidence closures. Bind receipt subject and accepted verdict; prove complete selected success at candidate/integration revision with admission; reconcile unrecorded merges without merging twice. Remove panel merge and add drift coverage.
  4. Worker activation and measurement. Join claim commits, admission results, governance records and receipts; build reset counter and packet-cost roll-up. Test competing/expired claims, stale writes and failed publication; separately test role handover and reviewed harvest/archive.
  5. Cost and hosts. Measure concurrency, timeouts, caching, idle/cascade removal and the always-on runner with hosted fallback before banking savings. Mini survival and restore proceed in parallel; only then a possible second runner.
  6. Exit. Prove equivalent forge execution, identity and result semantics, then complete cutover acceptance. Later Google exit follows chosen cost evidence.
