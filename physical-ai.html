Seitiate works seamlessly across your phone and smart glasses like the Ray-Ban Meta Display.

Most of life happens away from a desk—walking down the street, traveling, or talking with people face to face. Typing into a chat box on a computer is only a small part of how you actually think and work.

We are building Seitiate’s own companion experience for smart glasses like the Ray-Ban Meta Display alongside your phone. You can speak naturally to your Personal Assistant while you are on the move and see a calm, clear visual summary right in front of you—whether that is a quick reminder before a meeting, a decision from a shared workspace, or an idea you want to capture on the spot.

Because it is the exact same Personal Assistant that already knows your ongoing projects, preferences, and priorities, nothing gets lost between your glasses, your phone, and your desk. When you sit back down with your team, everything you captured or decided is already organized and waiting for you.
