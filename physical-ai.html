Beyond the Screen

Your Personal Assistant, right where you are

Seitiate is designed for the moments when you are away from a desk—walking between meetings, reviewing a studio cut, or working hands-free through smart glasses while your creative and professional workflows keep moving in the background.

Smart Glasses & In-Lens Companion

Wayfarer smart glasses with a quiet, glanceable in-lens card—giving you a calm prompt from your Personal Assistant without pulling you out of the room.

1. A quiet voice and a glanceable prompt

Most software demands your eyes and both hands. Seitiate works the other way around: your Personal Assistant already knows your ongoing projects, the people you work with, and the decisions waiting on you. Whether you speak a quick thought on a walk or glance at a single three-line card inside your glasses, your assistant captures the intent and routes the work silently behind the scenes.

2. Continuous memory from the street to the studio

There is no separate "mobile mode" to sync. A note you dictate while stepping out of a meeting immediately updates your shared workspace, drafts the follow-up in your voice, or queues a revision in your creative studio. When you sit back down at your desk, the work is already waiting for you.

3. You stay present; your assistant handles the machinery

Instead of noisy notifications, your assistant surfaces only what matters right now—a timely reminder of who you are meeting, a quick yes-or-no approval on a team deliverable, or a gentle cue that a long-running creative render has finished.
