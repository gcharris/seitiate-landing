01 · Hands-Free Presence

A quiet voice and a glanceable prompt

Most software demands your eyes and both hands. Seitiate works the other way around: your Personal Assistant already knows your ongoing projects, the people you work with, and the decisions waiting on you.

02 · Continuous Memory

From the street straight to the studio

There is no separate mobile mode to sync. A note you dictate while stepping out of a meeting immediately updates your shared workspace, drafts the follow-up in your voice, or queues a revision in your creative workbench.

03 · Calm Focus

You stay in the room; your assistant handles the rest

Instead of noisy notifications, your assistant surfaces only what matters right now—a timely reminder of who you are meeting, a quick yes-or-no approval for a collaborator, or a cue that a render has finished.
