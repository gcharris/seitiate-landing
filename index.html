The SEIT Credit & Membership Plans

How SEIT Connects the Platform (Launching at 1 SEIT = US$1.00)

The platform’s credit, SEIT, connects every activity across Seitiate:

  • Monthly Plans & Top-Ups: Members subscribe to monthly plans that include SEIT and can purchase additional credits by card or crypto.
  • Spend & Transfer Across the Platform: Members can transfer SEIT to other members and spend it on offerings within the platform—for example, a creator’s video channel, a specialist’s course, or an AI workflow or tool.
  • Creator & Builder Redemption: Members who earn SEIT can redeem it for crypto, with CryptoPass providing wallet verification.

Over time, SEIT’s value could change, and we may explore making it a cryptocurrency in its own right.
