01 / SEITY

Seity — keep what makes the work yours.

Your accumulated context, taste, style, judgment, and standards form a personal foundation that grows with you. It belongs to you. Your assistant can draw on that understanding across projects and conversations.

02 / ASSISTANT

Seitiate Assistant — keep the work on course.

An assistant grounded in your seity helps carry your intent through the work: coordinating tools, specialist agents, and collaborators around your priorities and standards.

03 / SEIT

SEIT — connect the work to an economy.

One common credit for buying, selling, subscribing, collaborating and earning. SEIT is designed to flow freely: in by card or crypto, across to other members, and back out to the wider financial world.

One credit connects the activity.

SEIT ties participation, trade and earnings into one economy. Plans come with SEIT, top-ups arrive by card or crypto, and what members earn can travel on — to another member's work, or out into crypto.

Earnings connect to the wider world.

What you earn here doesn't have to stay here. SEIT is built to reach beyond the platform, redeemable for crypto through verified wallets with CryptoPass. Its longer-term form stays open, up to and including a native token of its own.
