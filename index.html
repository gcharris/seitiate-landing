The SEIT Economy

One credit connects everything.

SEIT is the platform’s credit. It launches at 1 SEIT = US$1.00.

Launching at 1 SEIT = US$1.00
  • Subscribe and top up. Members choose a monthly plan that includes SEIT and can purchase additional credits by card or crypto at any time.
  • Spend across the platform. Transfer SEIT to other members and spend it on anything within Seitiate—a creator’s video channel, a specialist’s course, or an AI workflow or tool.
  • Earn and redeem. Members who earn SEIT can redeem it for crypto, with CryptoPass providing wallet verification.

Over time, SEIT’s value could change, and we may explore making it a cryptocurrency in its own right.
