01 / SEITY

The personal foundation.

Your seity is the growing body of context, preferences, judgment, and standards formed through your work and experience. It belongs to you. As the tools and models change, the understanding you accumulate remains the foundation for your work.

02 / ASSISTANT

Understanding put to work.

Your Seitiate Assistant draws on your seity to help you research, organise, create, and act. It connects your ideas to tools, shared workspaces, and other people—helping you develop something worth using, sharing, or selling.

03 / SEIT

A means of exchange.

SEIT connects participation, payments, and earnings across the ecosystem. Use it to access services, buy an offering, pay a collaborator, or earn from what you create. Funding and redemption connect that activity to the wider economy.

Seitiate brings these together on shared infrastructure: a personal foundation, an assistant that helps you act, and an economy in which people and businesses can trade.

The shared infrastructure

The foundation is shared.
What you build is yours.

Personal Assistants, shared workspaces, AI capabilities, accounts, subscriptions, and a common credit system provide the building blocks for many different experiences.

An individual can publish an offering. A specialist can turn a method into a tool. A team can develop an application or an independent business—with its own identity and customer relationships—on the same foundation.

The marketplace · powered by SEIT

A place to create.
A market to trade.

Buy a specialist’s course. Subscribe to a filmmaker’s channel. Pay for an AI workflow. Sell a tool you have built. Members can be customers, collaborators, and creators within the same ecosystem.

  1. Fund

  4. Earn

One credit connects the activity.

SEIT launches at 1 SEIT = US$1.00. Monthly plans include credits, with additional top-ups by card or crypto. Members can transfer SEIT to one another and spend it on content, services, tools, and applications.

Earnings connect to the wider world.

Members who earn SEIT can redeem it for crypto, with CryptoPass providing wallet verification. Over time, SEIT’s value could change, and we may explore making it a cryptocurrency in its own right.
