How the SEIT Platform Credit Works (Launching at 1 SEIT = US$1.00):
  • Monthly Plans & Top-Ups: Members subscribe to monthly plans that include SEIT and can purchase additional credits by card or crypto (or connect their own provider API keys via BYOK).
  • Spend & Transfer Across the Platform: Members can transfer SEIT to other members and spend it on offerings within the platform—such as a creator’s video channel, a specialist’s course, or an AI workflow or tool.
  • Creator & Builder Redemption: Members who earn SEIT can redeem it for crypto, with CryptoPass providing Know-Your-Wallet (KYW™) verification.
