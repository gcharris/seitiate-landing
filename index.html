How do you make AI work on your terms?

01 / SEITY

Seity — keep what makes the work yours.

Your accumulated context, taste, style, judgment, and standards form a personal foundation that grows with you. It belongs to you. Your assistant can draw on that understanding across projects and conversations.

02 / ASSISTANT

Seitiate Assistant — keep the work on course.

An assistant grounded in your seity helps carry your intent through the work: coordinating tools, specialist agents, and collaborators around your priorities and standards.

03 / SEIT

SEIT — connect the work to an economy.

A common platform credit lets people buy, sell, subscribe, collaborate, and earn. Card and crypto funding, transfers between members, and crypto redemption connect activity inside Seitiate with the wider financial world.

Seitiate provides the shared infrastructure that brings all three together—so people can build on their own understanding, put it to work, and participate in an economy around what they create.

The shared infrastructure

The foundation is shared.
What you build is yours.

Personal Assistants, shared workspaces, AI capabilities, accounts, subscriptions, and a common credit system provide the building blocks for many different experiences.

An individual can publish an offering. A specialist can turn a method into a tool. A team can develop an application or an independent business—with its own identity and customer relationships—on the same foundation.

The marketplace · powered by SEIT

A place to create.
A market to trade.

Buy a specialist’s course. Subscribe to a filmmaker’s channel. Pay for an AI workflow. Sell a tool you have built. Members can be customers, collaborators, and creators within the same ecosystem.

One credit connects the activity.

SEIT connects participation, trade, and earnings across the ecosystem. Monthly plans include SEIT, with additional top-ups by card or crypto. Members can spend and transfer SEIT, and redeem their earnings in crypto.

Earnings connect to the wider world.

Members who earn SEIT can redeem it for crypto, with CryptoPass providing wallet verification. SEIT’s denomination and longer-term form remain open, including the possibility of a native token.
